# GreeFin

![GreeFin Logo](https://github.com/bugrahankaramollaoglu/GreeFin/assets/160841695/08aef891-c713-4dd3-849c-1691051045f9)

**GreeFin** is an innovative mobile application that bridges the gap between financial management and environmental sustainability. It empowers users to manage their financial transactions while tracking and reducing their carbon footprint.

---

## Project Overview

GreeFin helps users become more conscious of their environmental impact by analyzing their daily spending habits. By integrating sustainability metrics into financial tracking, the app transforms traditional budgeting into a tool for a greener planet.

### Key Features
* **Income & Expense Tracking:** Easily log financial transactions manually.
* **Carbon Footprint Calculator:** Automatically calculates the carbon impact based on consumption data in categories like shopping, energy, and transportation.
* **Sustainability Analytics:** Visualizes monthly spending reports alongside carbon emission graphs.
* **Eco-Friendly Maps:** Provides route suggestions to identify the lowest-emission transportation methods between locations.
* **Sustainability Insights:** Offers tips and links to environmental projects to encourage a more sustainable lifestyle.

## Tech Stack

The project was developed using modern cross-platform technologies and tools:

* **Framework:** Flutter (Dart)
* **Backend & Database:** Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore)
* **Design Tools:** Figma & Canva
* **Project Management:** Trello & Scrum Methodology
* **State Management & Libraries:** Provider, Google Maps Flutter, Fl Chart

## Development Process (Scrum)

The project was completed over three Agile sprints:
* **Sprint 1:** Foundation, UI/UX design, and Firebase integration.
* **Sprint 2:** Implementation of the income/expense mechanism and core carbon calculation logic.
* **Sprint 3:** Finalizing the Carbon Page, Maps integration, and UI polishing.
